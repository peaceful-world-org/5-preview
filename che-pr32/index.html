THE SITUATION

A small nonprofit explains its mission on its website, but visitors struggle to see what it does and how to help.

LIKELY BOTTLENECK

The mission is described in detail, while the main action and evidence of impact are hard to find.

FIRST ACTIONS
  1. Explain the mission in one sentence people can understand.
  2. Make the options to help and the evidence of impact easy to find.
  3. Test the path with real visitors before spending on advertising.
OPTIONAL NEXT STEP

If helpful, discuss a small website and measurement project.

Why free? You can assess how we think before committing; we learn about a real problem before proposing a system.
