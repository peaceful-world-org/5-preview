2. What happens during normal use

You do not need to register to use the practice. The app does not ask for your name or email address, does not require an account, and contains no ads. Google Analytics loads only after you give separate, voluntary consent. Without your consent, Google Analytics does not load and no usage events are sent to it.

If you allow analytics, we use it only for general product statistics, including opening the app, starting and completing the practice, opening and successfully submitting the feedback form, starting the AI Guide, PWA installation actions, and using Share. Google Analytics may also automatically generate standard web events such as page_view, session_start, user_engagement and scroll, and process standard technical web-session parameters such as the page URL and title, browser/device type, approximate location and referral source. Along with our product events, the interface language, app version, and whether the app is running in a browser or as an installed PWA may be sent. We do not send feedback text or voice recordings, email addresses, AI Guide conversation content, or your local practice history through analytics.

Ad personalization and Google Signals are disabled for analytics. We do not use these events for advertising or to create advertising audiences. You can change your consent through the Privacy link on the app’s home screen.

Your browser may store your selected ticking setting, the number of practices you have fully completed, and your analytics choice. The counter is used to rotate wording in sequence and to show you the number of the practice you just completed. Your analytics choice is stored so the app can remember your decision and not show the consent dialog every time you open it. These local data are specific to this browser or device, do not create an account, are not used for streaks, rewards or advertising, and are not automatically sent to us.

If you voluntarily send feedback, the browser may also temporarily store a random submission identifier in sessionStorage. It exists only for the current browser session and is used to associate the submitted feedback for technical purposes.

To let the PWA work offline, the browser may also store a technical cache of the app files. Local settings and the cache stay on your device.

An optional AI Guide is available on the home and completion screens. To display its interface, the app loads an external ElevenLabs component; the widget’s technical file is delivered through unpkg infrastructure. With these network requests, the relevant providers may technically receive standard connection data such as your IP address, user-agent and the address of the requested resource.

3. AI Guide and ElevenLabs

The AI Guide is an optional feature for short questions about the practice. It does not run during the five minutes of the practice itself, and it is not required to complete the practice.

If you start a conversation with the AI Guide, the text of your messages, your voice input and the agent’s responses are processed by ElevenLabs infrastructure to provide the conversation. The microphone is used only after an explicit action from you and permission from the browser. If you do not start a conversation, the app does not send your voice or conversation text to ElevenLabs.

For the Peaceful World agent, storage of conversation audio is disabled: your voice is transmitted for real-time processing, but new call audio recordings are not stored. The conversation transcript and related data may be stored by ElevenLabs for no more than 30 days; the agent is configured to delete the transcript and derived fields containing personal data at the end of that period.

The Peaceful World workspace is opted out of the provider’s use of workspace data for training and improving its models. This does not prevent the technical processing needed to provide the service itself, maintain security, or meet ElevenLabs’ applicable legal obligations.

Access to the agent is restricted by an allowlist to the app’s main address, 5.peaceful-world.org, and the technical GitHub Pages host, peaceful-world-org.github.io. This reduces the risk of our public agent being used from unrelated websites.

AI Guide conversations are not sent to Supabase through our feedback form, and the app does not combine them with the local practice counter.

Do not share information with the AI Guide that you would not want to send to a third-party AI service. Responses are generated automatically and may contain errors. For more information about the provider’s own data practices, see the ElevenLabs Privacy Policy.

4. What data is sent with feedback

Sending feedback is voluntary. If you select Send, we may receive:

  • the text of your feedback;
  • a voice recording, if you record one and choose to send it;
  • the language, app version, copy version and identifier of the wording set you were shown;
  • the number of the practice you just completed on this device, for example “4”, so we can understand at what stage of use the feedback was submitted;
  • the page URL, a random submission identifier, and technical browser/device information (user-agent) needed for diagnostics;
  • your email address, only if you separately choose to help with testing from time to time.

We do not send a list of your previous practices, or their dates or times. With voluntary feedback, only the number of the practice you just completed on this device is sent. If the app data is cleared or you use a different device or browser, the local count starts again.

As with a normal network request, your IP address may be technically processed by Supabase infrastructure when the request is delivered. We do not add your IP address to the feedback record in our table.

Microphone access for voice feedback is requested only after you start recording. A voice recording is not sent automatically: until you select Send, it stays on your device and you can delete it or record again. This is separate from a voice conversation with the AI Guide.

5. Why we use this data

We use feedback and minimal technical data to find errors and improve the exercises, wording, translations and operation of the app. The completed-practice number helps us distinguish a new user’s first impressions from feedback after repeated use. Our legal basis for processing text feedback and technical data is our legitimate interest in improving and protecting the product.

If you allow Google Analytics, we use analytics events to understand how people use the product overall: how many users open the app, start and complete practices, return, install the PWA, use the AI Guide, share the app and leave feedback. The legal basis for this analytics processing is your consent. Without your consent, the analytics tag does not load.

A voice recording submitted through the feedback form is processed on the basis of your consent, which you give by voluntarily sending the recording after the app shows information about how the data will be processed. You can withdraw your consent by contacting us and asking us to delete the recording.

If you separately provide your email address to take part in testing, we use it only for short questions and invitations to product research. This is not a newsletter. This use is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

The AI Guide processes the text or voice you provide only to give the conversational response you requested and to help you navigate Peaceful World materials. We do not use this feature for advertising or to make decisions about you.

6. Where data is stored

Feedback is stored in Supabase infrastructure. The project’s primary region is Frankfurt, Germany (EU, eu-central-1). Voice feedback recordings are stored in private storage and are not made publicly available. The feedback and contact tables cannot be read publicly; access is limited to the app’s server-side components and people who need it for development, research or support.

Supabase acts as an infrastructure provider and may use its own subprocessors. If providing the service requires data to be processed outside the selected region, the safeguards required by applicable contracts and law are used.

If you allow analytics, event data is processed by Google Analytics through Google infrastructure. During a network connection, Google may technically receive standard connection and device data. Advertising signals and personalization are disabled in our integration. For more information about Google’s data practices, see the Google Privacy Policy.

AI Guide conversations are processed separately by ElevenLabs infrastructure and its technology providers. They are not stored in our Supabase feedback table simply because you spoke with the AI Guide.

7. How long we keep data

We keep feedback only for as long as it is needed for analysis, improving the app and documenting decisions we have made. After that, we delete or anonymize it. Voice feedback recordings may be deleted earlier after the analysis is complete. A contact email address provided for testing is kept until you withdraw your consent or while participation in the research remains relevant.

The retention period for analytics data is set in the relevant Google Analytics property and may be changed by us in its administrative settings. If you withdraw consent in the app, new analytics events stop being sent from that browser, and Google Analytics cookies that the app can access are deleted.

For the AI Guide, storage of new conversation audio recordings is disabled. Transcripts and related derived fields in ElevenLabs are configured to be kept for no more than 30 days and then deleted. These retention settings apply separately from the Peaceful World feedback form.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, where applicable, you may request access to your data, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, request restriction of processing, object to processing, receive a portable copy of your data, and withdraw consent you previously gave. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

You can change your Google Analytics consent directly in the app through the Privacy link on the home screen. For other requests, contact us at hello@peaceful-world.org. If your request concerns an AI Guide conversation, say so and, if possible, provide the approximate time of the conversation; this will help locate the relevant data in the provider’s service. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection authority. For our Estonian organization, that authority is Andmekaitse Inspektsioon.

9. Children

The app is not specifically designed for children and does not ask for age. Please do not send personal data about minors through the feedback form or to the AI Guide without an adult being involved where the law requires that involvement.

10. Automated decisions and sale of data

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising. The AI Guide automatically generates informational responses, but Peaceful World does not use AI Guide conversations to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

11. Changes to this policy

If the way we process data changes significantly, we will update this page and the date below before the new rules start to apply.
