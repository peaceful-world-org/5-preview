Peaceful World 5 · personal pilot

This is not a public release. Sign-in is restricted to the pre-authorized test account. A separate Supabase test server is used. Practice history, selected dates and the account email are not transferred to the released app. You can turn off recording of practices and dates; signing in is optional.

History is saved in this browser and synchronized with the test server after sign-in. You can download an export under My progress. In this pilot, access revocation and deletion of test data are available on request. For deletion or questions about data processing: hello@peaceful-world.org.
