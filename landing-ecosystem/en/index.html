01 of 5

Stop

Choose something easy to focus on: your breathing, an object, a sound, or a feeling in your body. Focus on it for one minute. If you get distracted, gently come back to it.

03

What we know — and what we’re testing

We are testing a simple hypothesis: a familiar sequence may be easier to recall in real situations. We do not yet have evidence that these exact five steps change real-world behaviour.

What we can say

The practice gives you a short, repeatable structure for attention, goodwill, harm reduction, and a concrete next choice.

What we do not claim

Five minutes do not make a person peaceful, replace therapy, or solve structural causes of violence.

Safety first

Nonviolence does not mean tolerating harm. In danger, distance, protection, help, and accountability come first. You can also stop the practice if it causes strong discomfort.
